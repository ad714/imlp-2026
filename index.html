Event Guidelines

Setting the stage for every idea

A few simple practices that keep the afternoon focused, fair and on time.

  1. 01

    Take the stage in turn

    Present in your scheduled order so each team gets its full moment.

  2. 02

    Come ready

    Have your slides, files and props set up and checked before your slot.

  3. 03

    Own your time

    Finish within your allotted minutes so the programme runs smoothly for all.

  4. 04

    Stay in sync with the coordinators

    Follow Vanguard's cues for transitions, setup and Q&A.

  5. 05

    Listen as you'd like to be heard

    Give every team your full attention, and engage with their ideas.
